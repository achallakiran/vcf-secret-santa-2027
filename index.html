GAMEPLAY GUIDE

🎮 How to Move & Play

👆

Walking & Pathfinding

Click or tap anywhere on the carpet or tiles. Your character uses smart pathfinding to navigate around desks, chairs, and festive decor.

⌨️ Desktop: Use W A S D or Arrow keys to walk!
📱 Phone: Drag the on-screen Virtual Joystick (bottom-left) to walk smoothly!
🔄

Look Around & Zoom

Click and drag with your mouse (or swipe on touchscreens) to orbit the camera. Use mouse wheel or pinch two fingers to zoom in and out.

👥

Visiting Colleagues

Click on any coworker in the office! Your avatar will walk over, face them, and exchange a cheerful wave and greeting bubble.

🎭

Express Yourself

Use the action dock at the bottom-right or keyboard shortcuts 1–4 to Wave, Dance, Clap, or Cheer. Nearby coworkers will often celebrate with you!

🎁

Santa Hub & Clues

Tap 🎁 Santa Hub at any time to update your wishlist, privately reveal your Secret Child, and assign holiday tasks. Tap 💡 to reveal clues about who your own Santa might be!
THE SPIRIT OF CHRISTMAS

📜 Secret Santa Rules

🎁 ₹500 Budget

We recommend keeping your gift around ₹500. The real goal is thoughtfulness and holiday delight, not expensive purchases!

  • 🤫 True Anonymity: Only you know who your Secret Child is. Keep it a surprise until the Grand Finale!
  • 📝 Check Wishlists: Visit the Santa Hub to read what your Child wished for and craft a heartfelt Christmas message.
  • 🎯 Daily Tasks: As Santa, you can optionally assign fun, light-hearted daily holiday tasks to your Child.
  • 🔎 Office Clues: Every 24 hours, you can unlock a new clue from the 💡 bulb based on your Santa's physical office surroundings.
  • 🎭 The Finale Stage Show: The organizer will host the Grand Finale ceremony where Santas are revealed and gifts exchanged!

SECRECY & TRADITIONS

Core Rules of the Game

  • 🤫 100% Anonymity: You only know who your Secret Child is. Keep your identity a mystery until the Grand Finale Stage Show!
  • 📝 Check Their Wishlist: Head to Santa Hub to read what gift ideas your Child shared.
  • 🎯 Assign Holiday Tasks: Add fun, light-hearted festive challenges (e.g., "Sing Jingle Bells at lunch", "Wear red socks tomorrow").
  • 💡 Unlock Office Hints: Daily clues unlock every 24 hours based on where your Santa sits in the office.
  • 🎭 The Grand Stage Finale: The event organizer brings everyone to the Christmas stage to reveal the Santas and exchange gifts!
